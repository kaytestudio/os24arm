The desktop
Move an object: drag it with the left mouse button and drop it where you want it. The desktop remembers where its objects are.

Shadows: drop a file, a folder or a program from a folder window onto an empty part of the desktop, and a shadow of it is made there - a link to the original, its title in a lighter blue. Opening a shadow opens the original; right-click it for "Open folder of original". Dropping a shadow on the Shredder deletes only the shadow.

The Startup folder (in OS/2 System): the shadows you drop into it are started every time the desktop starts.

The Settings notebook: right-click an empty part of the desktop and choose Properties. The Background page sets the desktop's colour and pattern, and whether the version shows at the bottom left (the system's build and the Workplace Shell's); the Window page sets the title bars' colours. Changes show at once.

The desktop is kept as you leave it - where its objects are, its shadows, the folder windows that are open and where they are, and your settings - in \OS2\WPS.INI on drive D.
